How BriefGuard works

A four-step workflow for checking AI-generated work.

01

Capture the brief

Start with the original request. Specific limits and deliverables make stronger checks.

02

Make requirements visible

Turn the request into an editable checklist before you judge the result.

03

Inspect the evidence

Each finding includes an excerpt and a transparent status for human review.

04

Repair the gap

Copy a focused prompt that preserves satisfied constraints and names the missing ones.

◎

Trust & limitations

Useful checks should make their limits visible.

What this prototype does

Uses transparent, local text patterns to surface likely matches, explicit contradictions and missing evidence. You can edit the checklist and export the report.

What it cannot prove

A matching phrase does not guarantee a factual claim is true. This prototype does not verify nutrition, prices, legal requirements, medical advice or external sources. “Needs review” is intentional.

Privacy by default

The demo does not send your brief to an external AI service. Text is processed in your browser. Avoid pasting sensitive material into any demo environment.
